# Classroom Artwork Credits

Engineering Essentials cog artwork and Design And Modeling cube artwork are the owner's existing independently authored course graphics. Their geometry is preserved here. The classroom dial caliper is adapted from the owner's original photo-based schematic; it is decorative, not to scale or a validated model.

The lever illustration is rendered from the independently authored **Levers: Load, Effort, And Distance** game's procedural apparatus. It is not the VEX lever assembly from a curriculum packet.

The individual VEX IQ beam, angle beam and connector pin illustration is rendered from VEX Robotics part geometry already used by LeverWorkshop. Geometry remains VEX's; this project does not claim authorship or an open-source license over it. VEX and VEX IQ are trademarks of their respective owner. This independent classroom project is not affiliated with or endorsed by VEX Robotics. Pastel materials and the individual-part arrangement are new decorative presentation, not assembly instructions.

No PLTW packet illustrations, photographs, screenshots, wording, logos, or complete lever construction have been included. The repository's `docs/CLASSROOM_ART.md` records the exact sources and reproduction steps.
